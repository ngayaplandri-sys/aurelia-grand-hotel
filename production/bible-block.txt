AURELIA GRAND, a fictional contemporary luxury grand hotel and resort on a sea-facing Mediterranean headland. Architecture: five storeys of pale honed travertine and warm sand limestone, stepped terraces with deep overhangs, a repeated rhythm of tall rounded arches, slender dark-bronze window frames, a double-height arched entrance portal with tall bronze-and-glass doors and bronze lanterns, a long dark-bronze pergola on the roof, clipped cypress trees, old olive trees, umbrella pines. Interiors: honed travertine floors with fine bronze inlay lines, warm lime-plaster walls, fluted smoked-oak panelling, bronze details, rounded ivory bouclé and cream linen furniture, walnut and travertine tables, a monumental sculptural chandelier of hand-blown amber-glass globes on bronze rods, abstract art in sand and bronze tones. Staff: tailored charcoal jackets with discreet bronze buttons. Palette: ivory, sand, champagne, bronze, warm brown, black. Light: warm golden hour and soft tungsten glow, calm and quiet. Style: editorial architectural photography for a luxury travel magazine, 24mm tilt-shift lens, perfectly straight verticals, natural colours, rich fine detail, subtle film grain.
